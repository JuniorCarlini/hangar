# Logos

The `logo.svg` files of postgres, redis-cache, redis-queue, mariadb, mongodb,
n8n, uptime-kuma, rustfs, adminer, rabbitmq and meilisearch come from
[Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org) 16.35.0 (CC0 1.0), with the brand
color added as `fill`. The logos of valkey-cache, valkey-queue and pgadmin
are generic drawings made for Hangar (no brand mark).

The product names and logos are trademarks of their respective owners. They
are shown only to identify the software each template installs; Hangar is
not affiliated with or endorsed by them. Some owners publish usage rules
(for example RustFS: https://docs.rustfs.com/trademark/).
